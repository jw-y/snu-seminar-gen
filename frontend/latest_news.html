SNU Data Science Seminar “Bridging Precision Oncology and Digital Pathology: From Biomarker Prediction Models to Real-World Applications” Time : 09:00 - 10:30, Oct 21, 2026 Zoom: https://snu-ac-kr.zoom.us/j/2507466555 Abstract Next-generation sequencing (NGS) provides critical insights for patient care, yet limited accessibility remains a significant barrier. In contrast, hematoxylin and eosin (H&E)-stained pathology slides are ubiquitous across clinical workflows—offering a powerful opportunity to broaden access to precision medicine. This talk highlights key advances in digital pathology models that predict genomic biomarkers directly from H&E whole slide images. I will present the clinical validation of endometrial and NSCLC models developed as CAP/CLIA-compliant laboratory-developed tests (LDTs), as well as real-world applications leveraging foundation models and agentic workflows. Sun Hae Hong Sun Hae Hong is an AI scientist driving innovation at the intersection of healthcare, digital pathology, and genomics modeling. At Tempus Al, she led cross- functional initiatives to validate H&E-based biomarker prediction models for clinical application. Expanding beyond diagnostic prediction, Sun Hae leverages foundation models and agentic workflows to automate and accelerate scientific discovery processes, advancing precision medicine from algorithm development to clinical deployment.
