This talk presents Self-Improving methods and EXAONE Foundry, a closed-loop platform for building Enterprise Al agents by unifying the full intelligence lifecycle: from generating and evaluating domain-specific training data, to automatically searching for effective hyperparameters and training configurations, designing agentic workflows with system architectures, and continuously assessing their performance. Its central goal is to close two intertwined gaps: the gap between what human experts can explicitly articulate and the tacit knowledge embedded in their judgments, and the gap between that expertise and the cognitive limits that prevent humans from exhaustively exploring data, models, workflows, and evaluation strategies. EXAONE Foundry learns task-specific metrics, rubrics, and reward signals from expert demonstrations, evaluation traces, and procedural decisions, then uses them to iteratively improve not only the model, but also the surrounding data pipeline, training process, tool-use policy, memory, and multi-agent system itself. By turning implicit expertise into scalable supervision and every system outcome into new learning experience, EXAONE Foundry moves toward AI agents that can identify their own limitations and recursively refine how they learn and act.
