SNU Data Science Seminar “Interpretable AI for Mapping Developmental Potential in Health and Cancer” Time : 09:00 - 10:30, Sep 30, 2026 Zoom: https://snu-ac-kr.zoom.us/j/2507466555 Abstract Single-cell RNA sequencing (sCRNA-seq) has transformed our ability to study cell fate and differentiation. However, identifying the molecular hallmarks of developmental potential— the capacity of a cell to differentiate into other cell types—remains a major challenge. We introduce CytoTRACE 2, an interpretable deep-learning framework that characterizes cellular potency and differentiation states on an absolute scale from scRNA-seq data. Across 33 human and mouse datasets spanning 18 tissue types, CytoTRACE 2 consistently outperformed existing methods in recovering experimentally defined potency levels and differentiation states across the full range of cellular ontogeny. Beyond improved inference, CytoTRACE 2 revealed pan-tissue gene expression programs associated with developmental potential and enabled new insights into cellular plasticity. Applying this framework to cancer datasets uncovered tumor cell phenotypes linked to patient survival and resistance to immunotherapy. Together, these results provide a broadly applicable computational framework for mapping single-cell differentiation landscapes and offer new insights into developmental potential in both healthy tissues and cancer.
